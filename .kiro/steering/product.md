# Product: First Answer Pages

## What It Is

First Answer Pages is a free local business directory that brings the printed phone book back from the dead — rebuilt for the AI era.

The aesthetic is deliberately old-school: paper-yellow background, bold black listings, category headings, two-column layouts. The experience should feel like paging through a classic phone book from 1994, except it loads in a browser and AI assistants can read every entry.

**Tagline concept:** "The phone book is back — and AI can read it"

## The Problem It Solves

Millions of small local businesses are invisible online. They have no website. No Google Business profile. No Yelp page. They exist only as a phone number on a fridge magnet or a word-of-mouth referral. When someone asks an AI assistant "who's a good plumber in Nashville," these businesses don't appear — because they're not in any structured, machine-readable source.

First Answer Pages gives every one of these businesses a free listing page that any AI assistant, search crawler, or person can find and read.

## Who It Is For

**Businesses:** Local small businesses with no web presence — plumbers, electricians, hair salons, auto repair shops, restaurants, lawyers, landscapers, and any other category that used to be in the old printed phone book. The signup process must be dead simple. No tech skills required.

**People searching:** Anyone looking for a local service provider who wants a no-nonsense answer: name, phone number, hours, what they do, where they serve.

**AI assistants and crawlers:** The directory is structured so that AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google, etc.) can read and cite listings without friction.

## Core Listing Fields

Every business listing includes:
- Business name
- Category (classic phone-book style)
- Phone number
- Hours (per-day schedule)
- Services offered (list)
- Service area (city or region)
- Short description (plain sentences, no marketing speak)
- City and state
- Street address (optional)

## Tone and Voice

- Plain American English
- No hype, no marketing speak, no buzzwords
- Direct and factual — like a phone-book entry, not a landing page
- "We fix pipes" not "We deliver world-class plumbing solutions"

## What It Is Not

- Not a social network for businesses
- Not a job board
- Not a national directory — focused on local, city-level discovery

Note: Simple 1–5 star ratings and optional paid featured listings are planned for future specs. The free listing always remains free.
