# Design: the aged phone book

Applies to every page and every stylesheet change.

- The look is the owner's original design: a book-style serif, a big slanted headline, near-black ink, cream paper, a navy button. Do not switch headings to a blocky sans or to all capitals.
- Fonts are served from this site only (never Google Fonts or another host):
  - "Headline Serif" = Playfair Display 700 (slanted for page titles)
  - "Book Serif" = Source Serif 4 (text and buttons)
  - Fira Sans Condensed only inside form fields
  - "Old Numbers" = Rozha One, digits only (unicode-range), first in the stack wherever a number is the point: phone numbers, the score, numbered lists. Not in running text or the hours table.
- Aged paper: grain, soft blotches and browned edges that stay put while scrolling. Edges never cover text, and text keeps WCAG AA contrast on the paper.
- Phone-book pieces: dot leaders from name to number, double rules around section heads, 3 columns on wide screens, Verified as a boxed display ad, a blue Verified rubber stamp, a red confirmed stamp, the Founding Members box as a dashed clip-out coupon.
- No sideways scroll from 320 px up. Every button looks like a button and is at least 44 px tall.
- Never: "Yellow Pages", "YP", walking fingers, a yellow cover, or another company's logo.
