# Wording

Applies to every page, message and doc the app produces.

- American spelling (color, neighbor, license, center).
- Plain words. Short sentences. No hype, no buzzwords.
- Never use "Yellow Pages", or "YP" as a word. Say "local business directory" or "classic phone-book look".
- No dollar figures or estimates of lost sales. Every number shown is something we measured.
- Never promise or imply that a listing, a fix or a score change will make an AI assistant name a business.
- When talking about timing, say AI assistants pick up changes over "days to weeks".
- Do not use the word "guarantee".
