## V128 (Medium) The "food" rule is too wide (my V127 was wrong)
"dog food", "pet food", "cat food", "baby food", "frozen food", "organic food", "health food" all give "... food restaurants".
Add " restaurants" after "food" only when the word before it is on this fixed list: fast, soul, comfort, street, vegan, vegetarian, southern. A keep-capital cuisine word before "food" keeps the V123 rule ("Thai food" gives "Thai restaurants"). Any other phrase ending in "food" follows the normal rules and gets " businesses" ("pet food businesses"). "food" alone, in any capitals, gives "food businesses".

## V129 A few more fixed plural words
- "grocery" and a phrase ending in "grocery" give "... grocery stores" ("grocery stores", "Indian grocery stores").
- Add to the takes-"s" list: deli, steakhouse, grill, pub is there, diner is there.
- "steak house" gives "steak houses" (add "house" to takes-"s").
- "donuts", "doughnuts", "bagels", "cupcakes", "smoothies", "ice cream", "frozen yogurt", "Italian ice", "coffee", "tea", "boba" give "... shops": the plural words lose their final "s" ("donut shops", "bagel shops", "cupcake shops", "smoothie shops"), the others add " shops" ("ice cream shops", "coffee shops").
- "breakfast", "brunch", "lunch", "dinner" as the last word give "... restaurants".

## V130 Short abbreviations in an all-capitals phrase
"AC REPAIR", "TV REPAIR", "CPR TRAINING", "DUI LAWYER" lose their capitals. Add to the raise list (so they are capitals however typed): AC, TV, CPR, DUI, CDL, ATV, SEO, PC, EV, HOA, CBD, MRI, ER, UPS is a brand (leave out), AV. "ac repair" then gives "AC repair businesses".
