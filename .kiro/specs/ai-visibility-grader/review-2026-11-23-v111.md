## V119 (Medium) A plural abbreviation typed in capitals is broken
"CPAs" gives "cPAs" and "DJs" gives "dJs". `caseWord` sees a lower-case letter and lowers the first letter.
Fix: look the word up in the raise list by its lower-case form (with and without a final "s") before any lowering, so "CPAs", "cpas", "Cpas" all give "CPAs"; same for DJs, RVs, B&Bs.

## V120 Joining words are always lower case
"PLUMBERS AND ELECTRICIANS" gives "plumbers AND electricians". Any word whose lower-case form is on the joining-word list already in the code (and, or, of, the, for, in, on, at, by, to, with, a, an) is written in lower case, whatever was typed.

## V121 A phrase typed all in capitals
"LAWN CARE" stays "LAWN CARE businesses" because each word is 4 letters or fewer. When the phrase has two or more words and every letter in it is a capital, lower every word, then apply the raise list and the keep-capital list ("HVAC REPAIR" gives "HVAC repair", "MEXICAN RESTAURANT" gives "Mexican restaurants", "LAWN CARE" gives "lawn care"). A single all-capitals word keeps the 4-letters-or-fewer rule (HVAC, CPA, BBQ stay).

## V122 Keep-capital words get their capital whatever was typed

## V123 Food typed without "restaurant"
"X businesses" is wrong for food. Fixed rules, nothing general:
- The whole phrase is one keep-capital cuisine word, or ends in one ("Italian", "authentic Thai"): add " restaurants" ("Italian restaurants").
- The last word is "food" and the word before it is a keep-capital cuisine word ("Thai food", "Mexican food"): replace "food" with "restaurants" ("Thai restaurants").
- Last word on this fixed list gets " restaurants": BBQ, barbecue, sushi, seafood, tacos, burgers, wings, ramen, pho, steak. ("tacos" and "burgers" end in "s" but are not a plural of a business.)
- "pizza" gives "pizza places".
- "Bar & Grill" and "bar and grill" give "bar and grill restaurants".
Anything else keeps today's rules.
