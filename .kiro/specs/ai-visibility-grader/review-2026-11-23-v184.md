## V190 (must fix) "12am-12am" must mean open all day everywhere
grader.js about 520 (schemaHours) and about 586 (readableHours) read the whole range with `parseTwentyFourHourRange`, which skips the equal-times rule for 12-hour ranges. So the JSON-LD says open all day, but the Fix Kit label leaves the day out and the readable hours say "12:00 am to 12:00 am". Import `parseHoursRange` (grader.js about 26) and use it as the whole-range reader in both places. Tests: `schemaHours(['Mo-Fr 12am-12am'])` gives `['Mo-Fr 00:00-23:59']`; readableHours gives "Monday to Friday, 12:00 am to 11:59 pm".

## V191 Other equal times show as written
