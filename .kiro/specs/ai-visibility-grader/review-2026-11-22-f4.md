## V79. The service-area test misses common lines
For a Nashville Grade these real lines now fail, so the owner would be told to add a service-area line they already have:
- "Serving Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, Hendersonville and Nashville"
- "Serving homeowners and businesses throughout Middle Tennessee, including Nashville"
- "Proudly serving residential and commercial customers in Nashville"

The 80-character window was wrong only because it crossed sentences. So:
- Match on the text before punctuation is stripped, keeping the 200,000-character cut and the escaping of the city.
- Let the window after "serving", "we serve" and "proudly serve" run to the end of the sentence (a period, exclamation mark, question mark or line break), up to 120 characters. Use a bounded pattern, with no open-ended or lazy matching.
- Tests:
  - the three lines above pass for Nashville;
  - "We serve breakfast all day. Visit us at 12 Main St, Nashville, TN" still fails;
  - the designed examples still pass;
  - a 1 MB page of "serving " repeated stays under 200 ms.

These lines can keep failing for now, since they'd need a wider rule about regions and travel words: "Serving Middle Tennessee since 1998", "We come to you anywhere in Nashville", "Mobile service across Nashville". Don't add rules for them.
