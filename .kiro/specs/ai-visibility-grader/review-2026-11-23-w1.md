## V111 The second and third questions use the plural phrase only
Today: "I need ${plain} in City, ST. Which companies would you recommend?" gives "I need dentists in ...", "I need car wash in ...", "I need CPA in ...", and "Which companies" is wrong for a dentist or a restaurant. "the most reliable" is wrong for food and people.
Do not try to guess "a" or "an". Use `named` in all three questions:
1. "Who are the best ${named} in ${city}, ${state}? Name your top 5." (unchanged)
2. "I'm looking for ${named} in ${city}, ${state}. Which ones would you recommend?"
3. "Which ${named} in ${city}, ${state} do people recommend most?"
`plain` is no longer used in a question; keep it only if something else needs it, otherwise remove it and its tests.
These change the two extra questions for every service, clean ones included. That is intended. Old Grades keep their stored prompts and still show their answers (the report finds answers by the stored prompt).

## V112 "ian" is not a plural rule (code review Q1)
"Italian" gives "Who are the best Italians in Nashville, TN?". Same for Indian, Asian, Hawaiian, Brazilian, vegetarian as the last word.

Any other last word ending in "ian" gets " businesses" ("Italian businesses" is fine; "Italian restaurants" as typed stays as typed).

## V113 Words ending in "s" are not always plural

## V115 Capitals
- An all-capitals word is made lower case when it is 5 or more letters and letters only (PLUMBERS, ROOFING, ELECTRICIANS). It is kept when it is 4 letters or fewer, or holds a slash, a digit or "&" (HVAC, A/C, CPA, DJ, IT, BBQ, RV, B&B).
- A phrase typed in Title Case ("Plumbers and Electricians", "Heating & Air", "Mobile Detailing") is made lower case word by word, except keep-capital words and words kept by the rule above. Today only the first word is lowered ("plumbers and Electricians", "heating & Air").
- "a/c" typed in lower case becomes "A/C"; "hvac" becomes "HVAC"; "cpa" and "cpas" become "CPA" and "CPAs"; "dj" and "djs" become "DJ" and "DJs"; "bbq" becomes "BBQ"; "rv" becomes "RV"; "it" alone as a first word before "support" or "services" becomes "IT". A fixed list, pinned in tests.
- "Dr." is left as typed.
- "24 hour" stays as typed (no hyphen needed).
