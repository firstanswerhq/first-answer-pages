## V177 (must fix) 12-hour times without am/pm are read as 24-hour
`formStyleHours` (grader.js about 462) and `RANGE_24`/`from24` in `parseHoursRange` (schema.js about 144-151, 165) accept any H:MM-H:MM. So "8:00-5:00" (meaning 8 to 5) becomes "8am-5am" in the claim form, and an owner-typed "9:00-5:00" on a published listing now tells AI it closes at 5 in the morning. Wrong hours are worse than none.
Same rule in both places: treat a range as 24-hour only when it can't mean anything else — either hour has a leading zero ("07"), or either hour is 0 or 13 or more. Otherwise keep the value as found (formStyleHours) and return null (parseHoursRange). This matches the "could mean more than one thing" rule in `schemaTime` (grader.js about 480). Apply the same guard to `schemaHours`/`schemaTime` for the Fix Kit's label (older problem, same cause).
Tests: "8:00-5:00", "9:00 - 5:00" and "12:00-1:00" stay as found and parse to null; "8:00-17:00", "9:00-13:05" and "07:00-18:00" still convert and parse.

## V178 "00:00-00:00" must not mean closed all day
Google reads opens = closes = 00:00 as closed. `parseHoursRange` returns null when opens equals closes, except 00:00-00:00, which maps to 00:00-23:59 (open 24 hours). Test it.
