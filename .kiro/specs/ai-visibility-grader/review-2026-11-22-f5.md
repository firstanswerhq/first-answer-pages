## V80. Text split across tags reads as one sentence
A sentence ends only at a period, "!", "?" or a line break. Headings and list items usually have no period, so these pass as a service area for Nashville in no-storefront mode:
- `<p>We serve breakfast all day</p><p>Visit us at 12 Main St, Nashville, TN</p>`
- `<h2>We serve breakfast all day</h2><div>12 Main St Nashville</div>`
- `<li>Now serving lunch</li><li>Nashville location</li>`

When building the text the service-area test reads (`serviceAreaSource` in page-facts.js), put a line break where a block tag closes: `p`, `div`, `li`, `h1` to `h6`, `br`, `td`. Use bounded patterns, and keep the 200,000-character cut and the 200 ms bound.

Tests: the three pages above fail for Nashville; `<h2>Serving Nashville and nearby areas</h2>` and `<li>Serving Franklin, Brentwood and Nashville</li>` pass; a 1 MB page of `<p>serving</p>` stays under 200 ms.
