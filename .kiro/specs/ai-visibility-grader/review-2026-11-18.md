# Review of the Kiro rebuild of the AI Visibility Grader (Nov 18, 2026)

Two independent reviews were run in the cloud on branch `kiro-rebuild-grader` (after task 9.4, before R4), comparing the rebuilt files with the original code at commit 1ee205e. The full suite passes 1015/1015, so everything below is something the tests do not catch. Security came back clean: the address guard and safe fetcher are not looser than the original (checked on 300,000 random addresses and a fake transport), every user and AI value is escaped, signatures are compared in constant time, and nothing private is logged.

Work through the items in order. Add them to tasks.md as a new section "Review fixes" (V1, V2, ...) before changing code, then mark each one done. Read requirements.md and design.md for each item. No shell commands: the cloud runs the tests.

## A. Behavior (code review)

V1. Old stored Grades must keep working (the live site has Grades made by the original code in the same DATA_DIR).
- grade-html.js and grader.js read `grade.found`, which old Grades don't have. Fall back to `grade.facts` when `found` is missing, so old reports don't say "Add your phone/address/hours" when the facts have them, and Claim fills phone, address and hours.
- grader.js counts any answer without `a.second` as a main answer. Old Grades mark second opinions differently; after a recheck, an old Grade where only ChatGPT named the business went from 35 to 53 and said "Claude named your business". Second-opinion answers must never count toward the score or the "named" sentence, for old and new Grades.
- The `gr_` cookie's signed label changed from `requester:` to `ran:`, so people who already ran a check lose Google's live answer. Accept the old label too (or keep `requester:`).
- Old Grades have only `draftId`, no `draftIds`, so a returning browser gets a second Draft. Treat `draftId` as that browser's Draft when the cookie matches it.

V2. POST /grade must not wait on the Gemini name check. `start()` re-reads carried (reused) answers through Gemini one by one before it stores the Grade: 9 answers with a 1-second checker made start() take 9 seconds (the original took 4 ms). With the real 8-second timeout and up to 15 answers that is up to 2 minutes. Store the Grade first and re-read in the background, in parallel, like the original. Don't re-read answers on every reuse just because an earlier Gemini check failed.

V3. The two extra questions must run on unlock even when the free question failed or hit the daily limit (requirement 9.2). Today grader.js requires `aiMain.state === 'ok'`, so the unlocked report stays at 0 answers forever. Also stop the extra questions after the main assistant fails, so a paid call isn't wasted.

V4. "Check again" must not get stuck. `recheckRunning` has no time limit, so a restart during a recheck leaves "Checking your website again" on the report forever and the no-script page refreshes every 3 seconds without end. Expire it after 4 minutes, like `isStale`.

V5. A failed home page must not skip robots.txt and llms.txt. With a 403 bot challenge on the home page the new code says the AI crawler check was not checked and gives no score; the original still read robots.txt (score 0, "Blocked: ChatGPT (GPTBot).").

V6. Hours: the JSON-LD value "Mo-Fr 08:00-17:00, Sa 09:00-12:00" gives Monday to Friday 09:00-12:00. Parse each comma-separated part on its own. When unsure, leave the hours blank (the original did). In the fix kit's business label, don't put free text like "Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm" in `openingHours`; use the schema.org format or leave it out.

V7. Page title check (grade-checks.js) is wrong both ways: services with no word of 4+ letters always fail ("Best Spa in Nashville", "Nashville Gym", "DJ Services Nashville", "Nashville Bar & Grill"), and substring matching lets "Proofers Club Nashville" pass for "roofers". Match whole words, and allow short service words.

V8. Claim after the owner sent the Draft for review must go back to the same Draft (requirement 12.4), not create a new one. Today grader.js only reuses a Draft whose status is `draft`.

V9. Non-UTF-8 pages: safe-fetch.js ignores the charset, so a windows-1252 page shows "Caf�". Decode using the charset from the header or meta tag (latin1/windows-1252 at least). A lookup that throws synchronously must reject with a SafeFetchError, not a plain Error.

V10. Competitor names (name-match.js): nested detail lines like "Free Estimates" and "Same-Day Service Available" now show as competitors; and any name containing "Pros" is dropped, so "Plumbing Pros of Nashville" disappears (and a business with "Pros" in its own name loses its place). Fix both.

V11. Smaller: the unlock rate limit is used up before the form is checked (typos count toward the 5 per hour); count only valid submissions. With the grader switched off, /grade must still send the noindex header on its 404.

## B. Page wording and layout (read like a business owner)

Side-by-side screenshots were compared at desktop and phone width. Nothing overflows on phones and no wording rule is broken, but these regressed:

V12. Never sound like a promise that AI will name the business.
- Leave "AI assistants name your business" out of "Fix these first". The owner can't change it directly, and it was the only top fix for the large company, telling a business whose label and info sheet already pass to add both.
- Its fix must keep the closing words: "It can take days to weeks, and we can't promise AI will name you." Original full fix: "Claim your free listing and work through the fixes below. That gives AI more to read. It can take days to weeks, and we can't promise AI will name you."

V13. Lead form (report, gate on). "Tell us where to send it." is wrong: nothing is sent to the visitor (the lead email goes only to the admin). Say the report opens right away on this page, e.g. "Enter your name, email and phone to open the full report right away, on this page. It has every check, the fix for each one, and text you can copy and paste." Bring back the phone note: "Why the phone number? It is the fastest way to help if you want a hand with your report. We only call, text or email you if you check the box below. We never sell it."

V14. About page (/grade/about) is written for the wrong person. It opens "Someone typed your website into our free check, so our bot visited it.", but the reader is the person who clicked "What do we read, and why?" on the form. Under "What it does not do" it lists things it does. It must again say all of this (requirement-level disclosure):
- the server visits the website once: the home page, /robots.txt and /llms.txt; public pages only; no logging in, no forms, no scripts, no cookies; public internet only;
- the bot's user-agent string;
- we ask Claude up to three questions about the best businesses of that kind in that city; the questions name the kind of business and the city, never the business name;
- each answer, with the business name, city and state, is sent to Google's Gemini to help check whether the business is named;
- when we can, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity are asked the same questions to compare; only Claude's answers count toward the score;
- the person who ran a check can see Google's live answer; it is private to them, never saved, scored or shared;
- reports are not listed in search engines and are deleted after 90 days; how to ask for removal.

V15. Google's live answer on the report needs its section back: the heading "What Google's AI says", the question asked, and the note "From Google's Gemini. Google doesn't review or endorse this report. Only you can see the answer. We don't save it. Your score comes from Claude's answers only." The live answer page also dropped "Google doesn't review or endorse this report."

V16. Free listing box: the only line under it is "Nothing goes live until you do.", which makes no sense on its own. Bring back the full note: "A free listing for your business in our directory, First Answer Pages. Next you check the details and send it to us for approval. Nothing goes live until you do." Keep the heading "Your free listing in our directory" and the compact phone-book listing look (name and phone on one line, old-style phone digits) that the owner chose.

V17. "We just asked Claude" shows forever. After 10 minutes say "We asked <assistant> on <date>", like the original.

V18. Form errors look like the grey hints. Make errors red and bold again, bring back the top note "Please fix the problems below.", and remove the duplicate State hint ("2 letters, like TN" and "Use the 2-letter state, like TN.").

V19. Check titles must be neutral names, not claims: a failing check reads "Your street address is on your home page - NEEDS WORK". Use names like "Street address on the home page", "AI assistants are allowed in", "Page title names your city and service". Keep plain words in details: "Blocked: ChatGPT (GPTBot)." not "Blocked: GPTBot."; avoid jargon like "meta description" and "og:image" in owner-facing text.

V20. Bring back the help for owners who aren't computer people: "Not a computer person? Send this page to whoever built your website." under "Fix these first", and the fix-kit intro telling them to send the kit to whoever looks after their website.

V21. Smaller layout points:
- the score-by-area list needs its "Score by area" heading and points ("AI answers: 0 of 30 points"), not "AI answers: No.";
- "Check again" goes after the fix kit with its "Made some changes?" heading;
- hint lines (like the report-removal line) use the small hint style, not full body size;
- with no website, the "nothing to check again" note is a plain note, not a red error;
- the 404 page for an unknown report keeps "Reports are deleted after 90 days."

## Keep (better in the rebuild)
The listing preview and fix kit include hours found on the site; each fix-kit item says where it goes; the failed page has a "Change the details" link; the no-website notice is easier to see; the capitalized demo sentence; the OK-to-contact box stays ticked after an error; the admin cards' "Open the report" link.
