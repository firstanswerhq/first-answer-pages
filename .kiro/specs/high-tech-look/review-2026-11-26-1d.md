8. **Tab contrast fix** — PASS.  
   - Rule: `.book-tabs a.book-tab.is-on` (and :hover, :visited, :focus-visible) uses `color: var(--color-on-gold)`.

10. **No shell commands or banned patterns** — PASS. No `exec`, `spawn`, `child_process`, or shell invocations in test files.

11. **Pure-function tests** — PASS. The first 6 tests import pure functions from home-network.js and test them with simple inputs and assertions — no browser, no DOM, no fetch.
