The same three Claude answers are printed twice: under "What AI says" (answers 1-3 in full) and again under "Every answer" (with evidence lines). Fix: (a) put the score box and the map on page 1 (shrink the map or the score box); (b) delete the answer text from "What AI says" and keep only the one-line summary plus "Other businesses it named", leaving full text in "Every answer"; (c) cut the score-card list to failed or partial items only on print (passes as one line each). That alone should reach 8 pages.
2. **The "Prepared by" version still reads as First Answer's report.** Where: print-pb page 1 and the health list. The title box still says "REPORT · FIRST ANSWER PAGES"; the health map still has a "Free listing" bubble and rows "Listed on First Answer Pages: Not yet", "Verified stamp", "Listing matches your website"; "Prepared by Harbor Digital Marketing" is a tiny line above the box. Fix: with Prepared by set, (a) kicker becomes "Prepared by Harbor Digital Marketing" (move it into the box, same size as the kicker), (b) drop the Free-listing bubble and the four "i" rows from the map and list, (c) keep only the small footer "Powered by First Answer Pages". 
3. **Broken sentence in the print footer.** Where: last line of both print pages: "AI assistants pick up changes over days to weeks, so after you make fixes." (the on-screen version ends "...so run a full new check after you make fixes"; the link was stripped and the sentence was left broken). An agency would print this for a client. ## Should-fix

1. **Green check next to a problem.** Where: Customer basics (health list and map). "Tap-to-call phone number: Found a link, but not the number" shows a green check and the score card says PASS 4 of 4 with a tip saying we couldn't read the number. Same for "Street address ... Found, but not on one line" (PASS 3 of 3) while "Address matches Google" is "Not checked". Part of this is my fixture (hard-coded passes), but the health list can still show a green check beside "but not". Show "!" (partly) whenever the status text says "but". An agency will spot this in one minute.
2. **"Who AI named, across 5 answers" is the best block but is buried.** It sits after ~3 screens of health list. Judge sees the long health list first. Move "Who AI named" and "Sites AI used" directly under the score box (on screen and print). It reads well: "3 Claude answers plus 1 from ChatGPT and 1 from Perplexity", table Hollow Creek 5 of 5, Maple Street 5 of 5, you 3 of 5. Add one plain line under it: "Two competitors were named in every answer. You were named in 3 of 5."
3. **Evidence line is good but says "model claude-sonnet-4-5" in the middle of a sentence.** Where: every answer ("Evidence: Claude (model claude-sonnet-4-5), asked Nov 25, 2026, 7:23 PM CT, with a live web search."). It does give model, time and search, which is what an agency needs. Suggested: "Evidence: Claude claude-sonnet-4-5, Nov 25, 2026, 7:23 PM CT, live web search on." Also the report footer says "8:23 PM CST" while evidence says "7:23 PM CT" (the fixture clock) - use one time zone label everywhere.
4. **CSV is usable but mixes five tables in one sheet.** Header is first, names are readable (`engine`, `web_search`, `question`, `named`). Problems: (a) the same 13 items appear twice (section "check" with Pass/Needs work and section "health" with Yes/Found/Good/Needs work), with different status words; a pivot table will double count. Suggest dropping section "check" rows or renaming statuses to one vocabulary (Pass / Needs work / Not checked). (b) `date` column is filled only on answer rows and holds an ISO string with milliseconds; `check_date` is ISO UTC (`2026-11-26T02:23:00.063Z`), which differs from the date on the page. Suggest `2026-11-25 19:23` in Central time, no milliseconds. (c) The "named" rows put "5 of 5" in the `detail` column; give them their own columns `answers_naming`, `answers_total`. (d) The `sites_cited` column holds full URLs in answer rows but site rows put the domain in `item` and the URL in `sites_cited`: fine but undocumented. (e) 7 of 23 columns are blank on most rows. A second option: offer three small CSVs (checks, answers, sites).
5. **No-"Prepared by" CSV still carries a sales line:** row "Listed on First Answer Pages: Not yet. It's free, claim it below." The Prepared-by CSV correctly says just "Not yet". Fine for the free report, but an agency who forgets to set the name gets it. Low risk; just note it. Better: drop the whole Free listing group from the CSV in both cases.
6. **Thin on method, which is what an agency owner asks first.** The report says "we ask the same question 3 times" and shows a sample, which is honest, but it never says (a) there is one question per check, (b) what the 13 checks weigh and why AI answers are worth 30 of 100, (c) that results vary by day. Competitors (Profound, Peec, Otterly) track many prompts over time. Add a short "How we checked" footer paragraph on print: "One question, asked 3 times to Claude and once each to ChatGPT and Perplexity. AI answers change, so treat this as a snapshot, not a ranking." This also protects against overclaiming.
7. **On screen, the page cannot be handed to a client.** The top bar ("First Answer Pages / List your business free"), listing card, "Claim my free listing" button and "Want help?" remain. That is acceptable only if the agency is told to use Print/CSV. The "Share this report" box says "opens a clean page for printing" - add "Use this for clients; the web page above is for you."

## Nits

1. "Fix: Add the business label (the code is in Your fix kit, at the end of this report)": lower-case "your fix kit".
2. Heading "Who AI named, across 5 answers": in the display font the "5" renders as a small old-style numeral and looks like a letter; same for "the 2 lines" in "Search summary (the 2 lines under your link in Google)". Use lining numerals.
3. "The lights below also show extra checks that don't count toward it": "lights" is jargon. Suggested: "The map also shows extra checks that don't count toward your score."
4. "i = about your free listing, not part of your health." is cryptic once the listing rows are removed for agencies.
5. "Not sure how to add these? First Answer, the team behind First Answer Pages, can apply the fixes for you." Fine on screen; make sure it never prints in the agency version (verified: it doesn't).
6. "Not a computer person? Send this page to whoever built your website." Warm and clear for a shop owner; keep it. In the agency print it should be "Send this page to whoever looks after your website" (print already says this).
7. "Other businesses it named" repeats under every assistant on screen; fine, but with 5 answers the screen is long.
