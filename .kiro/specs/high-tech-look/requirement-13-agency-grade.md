# Requirement 13: Agency-grade report (owner, Nov 25: "a real tool that even an experienced agency owner would use")

Why: paid AI visibility tools used by agencies (Semrush One, Profound, Peec AI, Otterly.ai, Scrunch) are judged on: which AI engines are covered, citation intelligence (mentions vs. the sites AI actually used), where competitors get named instead of you (share of voice), what to fix, and client-ready reports. We already cover several engines, local signals and fixes. These items close the gaps, using data we already collect, with no new paid calls.

13.1 Share of voice. Under "What AI says", a table of every business named across all answers on this report (Claude x3, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, the extra questions): business name, how many answers named it, out of how many answers came back. The checked business is always a row (even at 0) and is highlighted. Use the existing competitor/listedNames parsing; never invent a name; skip answers that didn't come back. Label: "Who AI named, across N answers".
13.2 Sites AI used. Answers that came with sources (stored as `sources` on each answer) are listed as "Sites AI used to answer": each site's domain, how many answers cited it, and a link. Mark the business's own website when it appears. Below it, one line per site that was cited but where the business has no link from its website or listing ("AI read Yelp for this answer. Your website doesn't link to a Yelp page."), only for directories we already recognize in profile-links. If no answer had sources: "These answers didn't list their sources." Never show a source that wasn't in the stored data.
13.3 Evidence. For every answer, an "Evidence" line: the exact question asked, the AI and model name, the date and time asked, and whether it used live web search. Shown on the full report.
13.4 Download for clients. A "Download report" button on the full report gives a clean printable page (print stylesheet: no nav, no animation, map as a static picture, list and answers in full, page breaks between sections) via the browser's own print-to-PDF, plus a "Download data (CSV)" link with one row per health row and one row per answer (engine, question, named yes/no, date). Same data as the page; nothing extra.
13.5 Prepared by (light white-label). On the full report, an optional "Prepared by" field (business or agency name, 60 characters max, text only, no logo upload) that prints at the top of the downloaded report and the CSV. Stored with the report only; never shown on public pages; escaped everywhere.
13.6 Rules: no new paid calls; score unchanged; public SEO unchanged (reports stay noindex); no report content added only by JavaScript; sentences 160 characters or less, American spelling; nothing promising AI will name the business; tests for each item (counts, the checked business at 0, no sources, a source that is the business's own site, CSV columns, print stylesheet hides nav and animation, Prepared by escaped and length-capped).

Order: after Requirements 2.10, 11 and 12, and before Requirement 1 (site look). Requirements 10 and 9 move after Requirement 1 and are built only if time allows.

Later (not now): multi-client agency dashboard, scheduled tracking per client, custom question sets, sentiment, an API.
