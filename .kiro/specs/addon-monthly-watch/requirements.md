# Monthly watch (add-on 1, from the Nov 26, 2026 competitor research, rank 1)

Owner: Colby. Every AI tracker sells a re-check over time. This add-on re-asks AI once a month for a business that has claimed a listing, and tells the owner in plain words when AI stops naming the business, or starts.
Rules that still apply: `.kiro/steering/wording.md`, `grader-safety.md`, `rebuild.md`, `tech.md`, `structure.md`. Plain words. American spelling. No dollar figures. Never promise or imply that anything makes an AI assistant name a business. Say "days to weeks". Do not use the word "guarantee".
Do not touch the ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity second opinions (`askQuestion`'s second-opinion code stays as it is). Do not touch the progress bar, polling or reload code, or `public/js/grade.js`. `public/js/health-interact.js` stays under 16,000 bytes.

## Why
Today a report is a snapshot. The "Check again" button reads the website only and never asks AI again (`recheck()` in `server/lib/grader.js`). An owner who fixes things has no way to see, a month later, whether AI answers moved. AI answers also vary from one ask to the next, so the add-on must not shout about one odd answer.

## Requirements
1. Off by default. A new setting `WATCH_ENABLED` (`on` or `off`, default `off`) turns the whole add-on on. With it off there is no new page, no button, no timer, and `/watch` paths fall through to the site's 404. A bad value logs one warning and means `off`.
2. Who may start a watch: the full report's visitor (same access rule as the fix kit, `fullAccess`), only for a Grade that is `done`, and only when the business has claimed a listing (a Draft was made from this Grade, or a listing with the same name and city exists, as `listingInfoFor` decides). Otherwise the report shows nothing about the watch.
3. Opt-in is one small form in the full report: "Watch this every month". Fields: email (optional). Plain line above the button saying what happens: we ask AI the same question once a month, we tell you here (and by email if you give one) when AI stops naming your business or starts, and you can stop any time. Works with JavaScript off.
4. With an email, nothing is watched by email until the owner opens the confirm link we send ("Confirm" page, then a button, so a mail scanner cannot confirm by opening a link). With no email, the watch starts at once and the alert is the page only. The watch page address is long and random and is never indexed.
5. The first reading is the report's own answer (no new AI call). The next reading is due 30 days later, or `GRADE_AI_REPEAT_DAYS` days later when that is more than 30. A watch never makes a full check, so it never counts as one.
6. Each monthly reading asks the free question of the main assistant once (`freeQuestion(input)`, so the owner's `about` words are used), with no second opinions, no extra questions, no Google or Bing lookups and no website read. If a full check of the same business with a fresh AI answer was made in the last `GRADE_AI_REPEAT_DAYS` days, its answer is the reading and nothing new is asked.
7. Cost limits: a reading uses one call from the Grader's daily AI allowance (`aiBudget`, kind `grade`) and never runs when 25 or fewer calls are left that day, so new owners' checks come first. At most `WATCH_MAX_PER_DAY` readings run a day (default 10, a whole number from 0 to 200). `WATCH_MAX_ACTIVE` (default 200) caps active watches; at the cap the form says "Monthly watch is full right now" and starts nothing. A reading that cannot run (no calls left, assistant off, failed) is tried again the next day, at most 3 days, then recorded as "We couldn't check this month" and the next one is due in 30 days.
8. A change is reported only when it is confirmed. When a reading differs from the one before (named to not named, or the other way), ask once more the same day. If the second answer agrees, it is a change. If it does not, there is no change and nothing is sent. Report only these two changes; never a score, a rank or a percent.
9. The alert says, in plain words: what changed ("Claude did not name your business this time" or "Claude named your business this time"), the question asked, up to 5 businesses it named instead (or in addition), and the line "AI answers change from one time to the next, so this is one snapshot, not a ranking." It points to the watch page. Nothing in it says a fix will change the answer.
10. The watch page (`/watch/<id>`) shows the last 12 readings: date, "Named you" or "Did not name you", and who else was named. It shows the latest change on top, a "Stop watching" button, and, with an email, which address gets alerts. It is escaped everywhere and works without JavaScript.
11. Stop and unsubscribe: every email has a "Stop watching" link and the `List-Unsubscribe` header. The link opens a page with a button (GET changes nothing); the button deletes the watch and its email address. The one-click POST form of `List-Unsubscribe-Post` also deletes it. Stopping is never rate limited and needs no sign-in.
12. Email goes through the existing Resend notifier only when `NOTIFIER=resend`. With any other notifier the form hides the email field and says alerts show on the watch page only. No new paid service and no new dependency.
13. The email address is stored only in the watch record, is never logged, never shown to anyone but through the owner's own watch page (as "a@…" with the middle hidden), and is deleted when the watch stops. A watch with 3 failed sends in a row is paused and says so on its page.
14. Privacy and logs: logs hold only the watch id, step names, counts. Never the business, the email, the question or any answer. Answer text is never stored in the watch; only named yes/no, position, total and up to 5 competitor names per reading. Competitor names are untrusted text: stripped of control characters, 80 code points at most, escaped on every page, never linked.
15. Time: dates on the page and in email use the same date text the report uses; a day means the Chicago day (like `aiBudget`). The timer ticks once an hour and runs nothing while a check is being asked in the same process more than `GRADE_MAX_RUNNING` allows.
16. Rate limits for the new routes: start 5 per hour per client, confirm and stop 20 per hour per client (stop is only counted, never refused).
17. Tests as listed in design.md. The existing test suite must still pass except the lines named there.

## Rules that still apply (checks, not new work)
The `watches` collection is added to the data store's allow-list and to `structure.md` and `tech.md`. The address guard and safe fetcher are not used (no business website is fetched). Every page sends no referrer, is never cached and is `noindex`.

## Not in this change
No Verified-only gating (open question for the owner), no weekly option, no score, no trend chart, no website re-read, no Google or Bing lookups, no SMS, no admin page for watches, no change to the report's score, the second opinions, the progress bar or `public/js/grade.js`, no change to `GRADE_AI_REPEAT_DAYS` itself.
